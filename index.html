WOLF CLASH ARENA

Privacy Policy

Effective 2 November 2026 · App ID com.wolfclash.arena · Developer: Vignesh V

This policy explains what information the Wolf Clash Arena game for Android ("the app") handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Vignesh V, an individual developer ("I", "me").

The short version

  • I don't ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or account. There is no sign-in.
  • Your best score and boss level are saved only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • Players in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and can change that choice at any time.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your best score, your Alpha boss level and a counter used to space out full-screen ads. This stays on your device. It is never sent to me. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show optional rewarded ads (for example, to revive or to double your boss score) and occasional full-screen ads between runs. Ads never play during gameplay. According to Google, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. All of it is encrypted in transit:

DataWhat it includes
IP addressYour device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general location.
Product interactionsApp launches, taps and ad video views.
Diagnostic informationApp and SDK performance, such as launch time, hang rate and energy use.
Device identifiersThe Android advertising ID, the app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. I don't receive these identifiers myself. I only see aggregated reports in AdMob, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch, Google's consent form asks how your data may be used for ads. To change your choice later, tap Privacy options on the game's main menu.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings, under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Google → Ads, depending on the device.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You can always choose "No thanks".

Internet connection

The game needs an internet connection to play. To check that you are online, the app regularly makes a small request to Google's connectivity check service (and to Cloudflare as a fallback). The game screen also loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, these reveal your IP address to those services. The app sends no other information with them.

Children

Wolf Clash Arena is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact me and I will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

I keep no personal data on any server. Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage. For data held by Google for advertising, use the advertising ID controls above or Google's tools at myaccount.google.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
